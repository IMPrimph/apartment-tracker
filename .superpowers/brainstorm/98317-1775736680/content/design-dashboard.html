Design Rationale

  • Hero card (dark) — The apartment progress is THE number. It gets the biggest, most prominent treatment. Dark background makes it impossible to miss.
  • Bank Loan + Cash — stacked to the right of the hero. These are your two funding sources that add up to the total. Shown with remaining capacity.
  • EMI + Misc — secondary row, smaller. These are tracking numbers, not progress numbers. Important but not the first thing you look at.
  • No title/header — The app name "Apartment Cost Tracker" wastes prime real estate. You know what app you opened. The progress card IS the header.
  • On mobile — Hero card goes full width, Bank Loan + Cash stack below it, EMI + Misc stay side by side.
